What this tells the organizer
These counts are context, not a fairness score. They do not capture every personal constraint or the degree of inconvenience.

Organizer's review

Use this information to guide discussion, not to automatically reject a time. The organizer makes the final decision.

  1. Review individual boundaries. Check preferred hours and any hard restrictions.
  2. Consider previous meetings. Notice whether the same participants repeatedly accepted inconvenient times.
  3. Discuss alternatives. Ask participants about constraints before deciding.
  4. Confirm with the team. Record the decision after the group agrees.
Design principle. Equal rotation does not automatically mean fairness. Consider individual circumstances rather than relying only on counts.
