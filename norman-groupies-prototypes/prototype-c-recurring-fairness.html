05 Organizer's review

Use this information to guide discussion, not automatically reject a time.
1. Review individual boundaries
Check preferred hours and any hard restrictions.
2. Consider previous meetings
Notice whether the same participants repeatedly accepted inconvenient times.
3. Discuss alternatives
Ask participants about constraints before deciding.
4. Confirm with the team
Record the decision after the group agrees.
Design principle Equal rotation does not automatically mean fairness. Consider individual circumstances rather than relying only on counts.
